# Initial Concept: Job Search & Interview Loop Tracker

> A first, rough capture of ideas for a web app to help me manage my job search and interview loops. These notes aren't refined or prioritized yet. Details and open questions will come in a later phase.

## Overview

A web app that helps me get a job by keeping all of my job search activity in one place:

- the communications I get about jobs
- my active interview loops
- finding and vetting new opportunities

It should also let trusted people (like my wife or an exec assistant) see how things are going.

## Reading Communications

- The app should be able to see job-related emails I receive.
- It should do the same for LinkedIn messages and text messages.
- It should only look at job-related messages. Anything else is off limits.

## Writing Communications

- The app should be able to draft replies to any communication it can respond to, probably using my Claude account.
- The app must never send anything I haven't approved.

## Managing Interview Loops

- Keep track of where each interview loop stands.
- Show what's coming up in each loop and the date of each phase.
- Let me drill into any phase, upcoming or completed, to see what we know about that interview.
- Make it clear which loops are open and which are closed.
- Make it easy to see whether I have any actual offers.

## Managing Contacts

- The app should track the recruiters I'm talking to, possibly in a contacts section.

## Shared Visibility (Spouse / Exec Assistant)

- Anyone I give access to should be able to see progress on my loops.
- This access should start as view only, so they can follow what's going on and possibly make recommendations.
- These people should also be able to point the app at potential jobs they find.

## Finding New Job Opportunities

- The app should understand what I'm good at.
- The app should understand what I'm looking for.
- It should find jobs on any useful platform it knows about.
- It should also check incoming communications for opportunities and judge whether they're good fits.
- I should be able to review a list of jobs it proposes for me, plus the ones it plans to reject.
- I should be able to see quickly and clearly why each job was chosen or rejected.
- I should be able to tell the app why its choices were good or bad.
