# Get a Job Dude

A personal job-search companion: it tracks every hiring process the Owner is in, prepares them for each interview, and lets trusted people follow along.

## Language

### Hiring process

**Interview Loop**:
One hiring process at one company for one role, from the moment the company offers a first conversation until it ends in an offer decision, a rejection, a withdrawal, or the company going silent (ghosted), such as a no-show never rescheduled. Sending an application alone does not start one. Two roles at the same company are two Interview Loops.
_Avoid_: Application, pipeline, process

**Phase**:
One step of an Interview Loop, named as the company names it. A loop's Phases are its own, discovered as the loop unfolds rather than drawn from a template, and each is expected, scheduled, done, or skipped.
_Avoid_: Stage, round, step

**Phase Kind**:
An optional label saying what sort of step a Phase is (recruiter screen, technical screen, take-home, onsite, hiring manager, offer), drawn from a short, extendable list.
_Avoid_: Phase type, category

**Job Lead**:
A potential job worth looking at that is not yet an Interview Loop, including one the Owner has applied for without hearing back. The Owner promotes it to an Interview Loop once the company offers a first conversation.
_Avoid_: Opportunity, posting, prospect, application

**Interview**:
A single conversation with specific people, held as part of a Phase. A Phase has one or more Interviews; an onsite of five back-to-back sessions is one Phase with five Interviews.
_Avoid_: Meeting, session, call

### Preparation

**Itinerary**:
The time-ordered view of upcoming Interviews, scheduled Prep Tasks, and deadlines across all open Interview Loops.
_Avoid_: Agenda, schedule, timeline

**Prep Task**:
A concrete piece of preparation for a specific upcoming Interview, which may be scheduled for a time with an estimated duration, like two hours of coding exercises the day before.
_Avoid_: Todo, action item

**Debrief**:
The Owner's record, made right after an Interview, of who they met, what was asked, and how it went.
_Avoid_: Retro, recap, notes

### App suggestions

**Proposal**:
Something the app infers and offers the Owner to accept, edit, or reject, such as a Phase detected in an email or a new Job Lead. Nothing changes until the Owner accepts it.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, recommendation, detection

**Draft**:
An outgoing message the app has written that waits for the Owner's sign-off, and is never sent without it.
_Avoid_: Reply, suggested response

### People

**Owner**:
The job seeker whose search this is, and the single source of truth for its Interview Loops.
_Avoid_: User, candidate, admin

**Viewer**:
A trusted person the Owner grants access, who sees every Interview Loop and may add Notes and submit Job Leads but never changes a loop.
_Avoid_: Guest, follower, assistant

**Note**:
A remark a Viewer or the Owner attaches to an Interview Loop, Phase, or Interview, such as a recommendation or reminder.
_Avoid_: Comment, recommendation

**Contact**:
A person the Owner deals with during the search, such as a recruiter or a hiring manager. A Contact can be linked to many Interview Loops; an agency recruiter pitching roles at several client companies is one Contact.
_Avoid_: Lead, connection
